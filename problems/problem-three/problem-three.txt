3. Balance Transaction Ledger

Stripe maintains balances by applying a sequence of credits and debits.

You are given transactions in chronological order:

[
    {"type": "credit", "amount": 1000},
    {"type": "debit", "amount": 300},
    {"type": "credit", "amount": 200},
]

Implement:

def build_balance_history(transactions):
    ...

Return the account balance after each transaction.

For the example above, return:

[1000, 700, 900]

The account starts with a balance of 0.

Follow-up:
A debit that would make the balance negative should be rejected. Return both the balance history and the indexes of rejected transactions. Rejected transactions should not affect the balance.
